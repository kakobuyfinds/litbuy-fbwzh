What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a shopping research list used to organize product links, categories, prices, measurements and comparison notes. This page is an independent browsing guide, not an official Litbuy spreadsheet or a live inventory feed.

How do I use a Litbuy spreadsheet to compare products?

Start with a category, record the original product link and selected variant, then compare measurements, stated materials, available photos and estimated total cost. Recheck the original listing before ordering.

Does a spreadsheet listing prove authenticity or quality?

No. A listing, brand name or photograph does not by itself prove authenticity, condition or quality. Check seller information and supporting evidence, and use a qualified authentication service when needed.

Which costs should I include in a shopping budget?

Include the item price, domestic delivery, service and payment fees, international shipping, and applicable taxes or duties. Check which charges are already included to avoid counting them twice.

Where do the category links go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS category pages. Other navigation and call-to-action links lead to the CAFANS homepage.
